# Website Reference Comparison: CarbonZero and TasseiGō

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Reference: [CarbonZero — Carbon Footprint Calculator & Reduction Planner](https://carbon-footprint-calc-v5.vercel.app/).

CarbonZero combines a dramatic, data-led entry screen with a product-oriented navigation bar for Overview, Solutions, Calculator, Planner, and Drive Sync. Its content guides visitors from measuring emissions to exploring solutions and making a plan. The Solutions view adds useful discovery controls—search, scope and category filters, scale presets, comparisons, and expandable details. Its guided tour presents the workflow in steps and links each step to the relevant tool. [1]

TasseiGō already has a distinct fitness-tech identity, a four-part Discover–Operate–Engage–Grow story, audience-specific sections, and a recently added dark-mode switch. It should keep its editorial fitness layout and its established moss-and-lime palette. A compact four-step tour is the strongest transferable improvement: it makes the existing product story easier to scan, gives each audience a direct path into the relevant section, and adds useful interaction without pretending that booking, membership, or facility data are live.

The reference’s greenhouse-gas claims, climate visualizations, reduction calculator, investment figures, Google Drive export, and climate-specific terminology are not carried into TasseiGō. They solve a different problem and are not features in the supplied fitness pitch deck. The adaptation is limited to the interaction pattern and clear task progression, not the reference’s brand or product content.
